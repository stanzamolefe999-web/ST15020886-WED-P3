Our Story

Craft first, always.

Kalahari Roots started as a single market stall in 2021 and has grown into a small store built entirely around one idea: South African makers deserve a shopfront as good as their work.

Who we are

Kalahari Roots was founded in 2021 by a small team who kept meeting talented weavers, potters and woodworkers who had no easy way to sell beyond their local market. We began with a single stall selling woven baskets, and within a year had added ceramics, textiles and furnishings from partner artisans across the country.

Today we trade from a shopfront in Woodstock, Cape Town, and a weekend stall at the Oranjezicht City Farm Market. See what our makers have created.

To champion South African craftsmanship by connecting local artisans with customers who value authentic, handmade home décor.

To become the country's most trusted destination — online and in person — for locally made home décor.

What we stand for

  • 01Fair pricing for makers. Every artisan is paid a fair price before we set our own margin — never a race-to-the-bottom wholesale rate.
  • 02Materials with a story. Ilala palm, sisal, reclaimed wood and locally thrown clay — nothing mass-produced or imported.
  • 03Long relationships. We work with the same core group of artisans season after season, not a rotating catalogue of suppliers.
  • 04Honest descriptions. Every product page tells you who made it, where, and what it's made from.
